Popular Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your event.

What types of events do you organize?

We organize weddings, birthdays, concerts, engagement parties, corporate events, seminars, and private celebrations.

Do you provide decoration services?

Yes, we provide complete decoration services including stage setup, floral decoration, lighting, and luxury themes.

Can I customize my event package?

Absolutely! We create customized event packages according to your budget and requirements.

How early should I book my event?

We recommend booking at least 1 to 3 months before your event for better planning and arrangements.

Do you provide catering services?

Yes, we provide premium catering services with customizable menus for all types of events.

Do you organize destination weddings?

Yes, we also organize destination weddings and luxury outdoor events across different locations.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, bank transfer, debit cards, credit cards, and cash payments.

Can I visit your office before booking?

Yes, you are welcome to visit our office and discuss your event planning requirements personally.
